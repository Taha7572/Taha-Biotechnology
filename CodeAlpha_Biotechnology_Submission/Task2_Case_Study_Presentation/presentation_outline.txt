SLIDE 1: Biocon Limited — Biotechnology Case Study
CodeAlpha Biotechnology Internship
Case study of a biotechnology company, its innovation, applications, challenges and market impact.

SLIDE 2: Biocon is an Indian biotechnology company with operations spanning biopharmaceuticals, biosimilars, generics and research-led products.
The company has developed a large-scale biotechnology business model combining research, manufacturing and global commercialization.

SLIDE 3: A major focus is biosimilars: biologic medicines designed to be highly similar to already approved reference biologics.
Biosimilar development requires analytical characterization, process development, clinical evidence where required, regulatory review and reliable manufacturing.

SLIDE 4: Biocon Biologics develops and manufactures biosimilar medicines in areas including diabetes, oncology and immunology.
The platform depends on cell-line development, bioprocessing, purification, analytical testing, quality systems and regulated manufacturing.

SLIDE 5: Biologics can be highly effective but may be expensive and difficult to manufacture.
Biosimilars can expand treatment options and may support greater affordability and availability when approved and appropriately adopted.

SLIDE 6: Applications include insulin and other therapies for chronic disease, cancer treatment and immune-mediated conditions.
The value is strongest where high-cost biologic therapies create access barriers and where biosimilar competition can broaden supply.

SLIDE 7: Biocon has built an international biotechnology and biopharmaceutical presence.
Its biosimilar strategy contributes to the wider shift toward more accessible biologic medicines and demonstrates the growth of biotechnology manufacturing in India.

SLIDE 8: Key challenges include regulatory complexity, manufacturing consistency, large development costs, competition, supply-chain requirements and maintaining product quality at global scale.
Biologics also require strong cold-chain, analytical and quality-control systems.

SLIDE 9: Biopharmaceutical products must meet strict standards for quality, safety and efficacy.
Responsible development requires validated manufacturing processes, pharmacovigilance, transparent evidence and compliance with national and international regulatory requirements.

SLIDE 10: Growth opportunities include new biosimilars, improved manufacturing technologies, digital quality systems and expansion into additional therapeutic areas.
The company can also benefit from continued global demand for high-quality biologic medicines at more sustainable costs.

SLIDE 11: Biocon illustrates how biotechnology can move from research to industrial-scale healthcare products.
